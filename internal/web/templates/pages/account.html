About your account & recovery codes (security)
What this page is

Your own sign-in details and the controls to re-set up two-factor authentication (2FA) - the authenticator app that produces your six-digit login code.

Recovery codes

When you enroll, SopsHQ shows ten one-time recovery codes. Each lets you sign in once if you cannot use your authenticator. They are shown only at enrollment, so store them somewhere safe. If you are running low, re-enroll to get a fresh set.

Security note

Re-enrolling immediately invalidates your old authenticator and any unused recovery codes. Only PBKDF2 hashes of the codes are stored - the codes themselves are never saved, shown again, or logged. A lost authenticator never locks you out permanently: a recovery code, an admin reset, or the CLI escape hatch always restores access.

Re-enroll your authenticator

Lost your phone, switched authenticator apps, or running low on recovery codes? Re-enrolling issues a brand-new authenticator secret and a fresh set of recovery codes, and invalidates the old ones. You will scan a new QR and confirm a code to finish.
